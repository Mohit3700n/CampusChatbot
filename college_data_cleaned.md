## 4. Campus Layout & Physical Navigation
The main college building spans four floors and is divided into two primary wings: **A Wing** and **B Wing**. 

* **Ground Floor:**
  * **A Wing:** Houses the Central Workshop (equipped with 3D printing facilities, electric circuit soldering stations, and wood/metal laser cutting machines).
  * **Civil Department:** Located beside the workshop (spanning from the ground to the first floor) and is notably the only department featuring fully AC classrooms.
  * **Laboratories & Libraries:** The main Chemistry Lab and the huge central Library Information Centre are located here.
  * **Administrative Offices:** Includes the Accounts Section, Training and Placement (T&P) Cell office, and the Principal's Office.
* **First Floor:**
  * **A Wing:** Houses the Mechanical Engineering Department and the Computer Science (Data Science) students.
  * **Civil Department:** The upper level of the Civil engineering classrooms.
* **Second Floor:**
  * **A Wing:** Dedicated entirely to all First-Year (FE) student classrooms. 
  * **Other Departments:** Houses the AIML Department and the Electronics & Computer Science (ECS) Department.
* **Third Floor:**
  * **A Wing:** Dedicated to Diploma (Polytechnic) students.
  * **B Wing:** Houses the Information Technology (IT) Department classes and specialized laboratories.

## 5. Academic Progression & Campus Culture
* **First-Year (FE) Experience:** The first year features a common engineering curriculum, meaning students from all branches are mixed together in shared classrooms (on the 2nd Floor, A Wing) to build a foundational understanding of engineering sciences.
* **Second-Year (SE) Transition:** From the second year onwards, students transition into proper department-wise classes across the campus.
* **Inter-Departmental Subjects:** Even after moving to specific departments, certain core subjects remain common across allied branches for specific semesters.

## 6. Admissions, Eligibility Pathways & Fees
* **B.Tech Admission:** Admission is strictly based on MHT-CET scores  . Admissions are processed through the MHT-CET based CAP (Centralized Admission Process) — evident from site references to "IL Seats" (Institute Level), "ACAP" rounds, and CAP merit lists for B.Tech/Diploma/MBA/MCA admissions  .
* **First-Year Diploma Eligibility:** Students can apply immediately after passing their 10th standard (SSC).
* **Direct Second Year Diploma (Lateral Entry):** Students who have completed their 12th standard (HSC) or hold an ITI qualification are eligible for direct admission to the second year of the Diploma.
* **Diploma to Degree (DSE):** After graduating with an Engineering Diploma, students are eligible for lateral entry directly into the second year of the B.Tech degree programs.
* **Tuition Fees:** The tuition fee for the first year of AI/ML Engineering is 1,20,000 INR per year  . Exact tuition fee amounts are not published as text on the site — they exist only inside PDF fee-structure documents  .
* **Hostel Fees:** Hostel fees are completely separate and cost 50,000 INR per year  .

# Examination Structure

## Semester-wise Examination Pattern

Each semester consists of **three major examinations**:

1. **IAE Test 1**
2. **IAE Test 2**
3. **ESE (End Semester Examination)**

### Marks Distribution

| Examination | Full Marks | Weightage |
|---|---:|---:|
| IAE Test 1 | 20 | 20% |
| IAE Test 2 | 20 | 20% |
| ESE (End Semester Examination) | 60 | 60% |
| **Total** | **100** | **100%** |

## IAE Test 1

- **Exam Name:** Internal Assessment Examination – Test 1
- **Maximum Marks:** 20
- **Conducted:** During the first half of the semester
- **Purpose:** To assess students' understanding of the topics covered in the first part of the syllabus.

## IAE Test 2

- **Exam Name:** Internal Assessment Examination – Test 2
- **Maximum Marks:** 20
- **Conducted:** During the second half of the semester
- **Purpose:** To assess students' understanding of the topics covered after IAE Test 1.

## ESE Examination

- **Exam Name:** End Semester Examination (ESE)
- **Maximum Marks:** 60
- **Conducted:** At the end of each semester
- **Purpose:** To evaluate the student's overall understanding of the complete semester syllabus.

## Semester Total

The final marks for each subject are calculated out of **100 marks**.

**Formula:**

`Final Marks = IAE Test 1 (20) + IAE Test 2 (20) + ESE (60)`

### Example

| Examination | Student Marks | Maximum Marks |
|---|---:|---:|
| IAE Test 1 | 16 | 20 |
| IAE Test 2 | 17 | 20 |
| ESE | 48 | 60 |
| **Total** | **81** | **100** |

## 2. Campus Entry, Security & Parking Procedure

*(This section covers what a new student/visitor experiences physically entering the campus — useful for "how do I enter campus," "do I need my ID card," "can I park my bike" type queries.)*

* **ID Card is mandatory at the main gate.** Students must wear/carry their ID card visibly when entering the campus.
* **If a student does not have their ID card:**
  * The security guard/watchman stops them at the gate and asks for the ID card.
  * If the student cannot show it, the guard notes the entry in a security register.
  * If required, the guard calls the HOD or a concerned faculty member to verify the student's identity before allowing entry.
  * The student is also verbally warned about not carrying their ID card.
* **Two-wheeler entry:**
  * Security staff verify valid vehicle documents (RC book, driving license, etc.) at the gate for students/staff arriving on two-wheelers.
  * Once documents are verified, riders can use the in-campus parking facility.
* **General flow:** Gate → ID card check (or verification call if missing) → register entry (if applicable) → vehicle document check (if on a two-wheeler) → entry into campus / access to parking.

  # College Rules

## 1. Attendance Rule

The college is strict about student attendance.

- Every student must maintain **at least 75% attendance**.
- If a student's attendance is **below 75% in a particular subject**, the student is **not eligible to attempt the examination for that subject**.
- Examination eligibility is checked subject-wise based on the student's attendance.

### Hall Ticket Attendance Verification

The hall ticket is generally generated **1 to 2 days before the examination**.

Before generating the hall ticket, attendance eligibility is checked for every student and every subject using **Y/N status**:

- **Y (Yes):** Attendance is **greater than or equal to 75%**. The student is eligible to attempt the examination for that subject.
- **N (No):** Attendance is **below 75%**. The student is not eligible / not allowed to attempt the examination for that subject.

> **Important:** Attendance eligibility is checked separately for each subject.

---

## 2. Identity Card (ID Card) Rule

The college is strict about carrying and wearing the student identity card.

- Every student must **wear their college ID card while on campus**.
- Students must wear the ID card **from the time they enter through the college gate until they exit from the college/campus gate**.

### Visitors and Non-College Students

The following persons get campus entry through the applicable visitor identification process:

1. **Visitors**
2. **Students coming for admission**
3. **Students from other colleges participating in events on the campus**

Such persons may be required to obtain a **visitor sticker or visitor identity (ID) card** for entry.

---

## 3. Lecture Timing and Late Entry Rule

The college is strict about punctuality for lectures.

- Students are expected to reach lectures on time.
- If a student arrives approximately **20 to 25 minutes late**, either:
  - the student may **not be permitted to enter the lecture**, or
  - the student may be permitted to enter but **may not receive attendance for that lecture**.

---

## 4. Examination Reporting Time

- Every student must be present in the examination hall **15 minutes before the scheduled examination time**.
- If a student fails to report **15 minutes before the examination**, the student must obtain **permission with the required signature from the Examination Cell (E Cell)**.
- After obtaining the required permission, the student can attempt the examination.

> **Important:** Students should arrive before the required reporting time to avoid delays and the need for special permission.
