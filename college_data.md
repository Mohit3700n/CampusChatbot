# College Timings
* The regular college timing is 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM from Monday to Friday.
* On Saturdays, the college timing is 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM.
* Sunday is a strict holiday.

# Admission and Fees
* The tuition fee for the first year of AI/ML Engineering is 1,20,000 INR per year.
* Hostel fees are completely separate and cost 50,000 INR per year.
* Admission is strictly based on MHT-CET scores.

# Placements and Events
* The annual tech fest is called "TechSpark" and is held in the month of February.
* The highest placement package offered last year was 12 LPA.
